Interest & Calculations

VSL calculates interest using the principal amount, monthly interest rate and number of days passed.

Formula:
Interest = Principal × Monthly Rate × Days ÷ 3000

For example, ₹10,000 at 2% monthly interest for 30 days:
₹10,000 × 2 × 30 ÷ 3000 = ₹200.
Interest starts counting from the actual Date Borrowed recorded in VSL. The borrowing date itself is included as the first day.
Days Passed displays the number of calendar days counted from the borrowing date, including the borrowing date itself.
Interest Paid represents the interest amount already paid by the borrower. This amount is deducted from the calculated interest to determine the remaining interest.
Remaining Interest is calculated by subtracting Interest Paid from the total calculated interest.

Formula:
Remaining Interest = Calculated Interest − Interest Paid

The remaining interest cannot be less than zero.
Total represents the original principal amount plus the remaining interest.

Formula:
Total = Principal Amount + Remaining Interest
Interest may differ because of the recorded borrowing date, monthly interest rate, principal amount or Interest Paid value. Check these details and contact your VSL representative if something appears incorrect.

VSL Premium+

VSL Premium+ is an optional upgrade that provides additional display and customization features for your account.
Premium+ includes:

• View Borrowed By details.
• View Interest Rate.
• View Date Borrowed.
• View Days Passed.
• Use Interest Paid controls.
• Turn detail popups and hover effects on or off.
• Switch between Light and Dark (AMOLED) themes.
• Customize the VSL main colour.
Current Premium+ pricing:

Monthly: ₹160/month
Annual: ₹1,500/year (equivalent to ₹125/month).
